About Us

Welcome to Passport Photo Maker, an easy-to-use online photo preparation tool designed to help users create passport-size photo sheets quickly and conveniently.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to provide a simple tool that can be used by individual users, computer centers, cyber cafes and photo studios for preparing passport-size photo sheets.

What You Can Do

  • Upload a photo
  • Crop and position the photo
  • Zoom the photo
  • Create passport-size photos
  • Prepare multiple copies
  • Create 4×6 and A4 photo sheets
  • Download the prepared photo sheet

Easy to Use

The tool is designed with a simple interface so that users can prepare a photo without needing advanced photo-editing software.

Independent Tool

Passport Photo Maker is an independent online photo preparation tool. It is not affiliated with or officially connected to the Government of India, Passport Seva, any embassy, visa authority or other government organization.

Photo size, background and other requirements can vary depending on the passport, visa or application. Users should always check the official requirements of the organization where the photo will be submitted.
